# Using the landing page

The landing page is the first screen you see at the root of the site. You do not need an
account to use it.

## What you see

- **A title and a one-sentence description** of the National Pathology Test Catalogue.
- **A search box.** Type a test name or a code, then choose **Search** or press Enter.
- **Account actions.** These change with your sign-in state; see below.
- **Three cards** that say what the catalogue is, who maintains it, and how to contribute.

## Searching from the landing page

Type your words in **Search by test name or code**, then choose **Search**. You are taken to
the catalogue search page with your words already entered. Spaces at the start and end of
what you typed are ignored. If you leave the box empty, you go to the catalogue search page
with nothing entered.

> **Not yet implemented.** The catalogue search page is still a placeholder, so it does
> not list results yet. The landing page hands your words over correctly, and the results
> appear there when that screen lands.

You can also choose **Search the catalogue**, below the search box, to open the catalogue
search page without a query.

## Account actions

What you see depends on whether you are signed in:

- **Signed out:** **Register** and **Sign in**. See
  [Signing in, registering, and signing out](signing-in.md).
- **Signed in:** **Sign out**.
- **While your session is being restored, or when sign-in is unavailable:** no account
  actions. This stops a returning user from seeing links to register or sign in that they
  do not need, and stops a sign-in link appearing that cannot work.

## The cards

- **What the catalogue is** describes the catalogue and links to **About the catalogue**.
- **Who maintains it** names the organisations that curate and publish it.
- **How to contribute** tells you that registered members can propose new tests and
  amendments. If you are signed out, it links to **Register to contribute**. If you are
  signed in, it links to **My submissions**.

## Using it with a keyboard

Press Tab to move through the page in reading order: the search box, **Search**, the
account actions, **Search the catalogue**, then the links in the cards. A visible ring
shows where you are. Screen readers announce the search box as a search region.
