# NPTC Catalogue Maintenance Platform: terms of use

**Temporary text.** This version exists so the acceptance record can be built and tested. It
is not legal wording. Production replaces it with a later version before real contributors
sign up.

## 1. Using the platform

You may read the catalogue without an account. To propose or make a change, you sign in and
accept these terms.

## 2. What you contribute

You are responsible for the changes you submit. The platform records who made each change and
when.

## 3. Changes to these terms

When these terms change, the platform asks you to accept the new version before your next
contribution. You can still read the catalogue, sign out and close your account without
accepting.
